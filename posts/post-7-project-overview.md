Um assistente de diagnóstico para equipes de manutenção industrial — construído para quem conserta o equipamento, não para quem escreve o manual.

## EN

After explaining RAG and Graph RAG separately, I want to show the project that brought both concepts together into a real application: Graph RAG Agent.

A diagnostic assistant for industrial maintenance teams — built for the people who fix the equipment, not the people who wrote the manual.

💬 Describe the fault, not the part number
"The crusher's eccentric shaft is vibrating" — plain language, no need to know internal component codes.

🧭 Get the probable cause and the fix
Every answer names the cause and the corrective action, with the exact chain of relationships and the source manual.

❓ Asked for detail, not given a guess
If the question doesn't have enough detail, the agent asks which symptom you're observing — instead of picking one at random.

The knowledge base covers 4 industrial asset types (all synthetic data, simulating realistic documentation):
🔧 Vibrating Screen · 📦 Belt Conveyor · 🚛 Haul Truck · ⚙️ Jaw Crusher

Open source, no frameworks — built to really understand how it works under the hood. Link in the comments.
