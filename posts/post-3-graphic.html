How RAG Works

Three steps most frameworks hide under the hood — built here in plain Python.

1

Indexing

Split the document into chunks, each turned into a vector (embedding) that captures its meaning.

▼
2

Retrieval

The question becomes a vector too. Cosine similarity finds the chunks closest in meaning.

▼
3

Generation

Retrieved chunks go to the LLM under strict rules: answer only from this, and cite the source.

CTX→LLM
