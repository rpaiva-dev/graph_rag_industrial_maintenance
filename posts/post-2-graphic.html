Grounding an LLM in your data

What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation — an AI answers from your documents, not just what it memorized in training. Letter by letter:

R

Retrieval

Before answering, the system searches your documents for the passages most related to the question.

A

Augmented

The model's prompt is enriched with those passages — real material, attached to the question, not just memory.

G

Generation

The model writes the answer using only what it received, and cites exactly where it came from.

Without RAG Closed-book exam — the model guesses confidently from memory.
With RAG Open-book exam — it answers only from the pages it just retrieved.
